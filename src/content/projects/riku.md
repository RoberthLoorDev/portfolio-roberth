body: |-
  This project focuses on the continuous development, stabilization, and modernization of <strong class="focus">Riku</strong>, a comprehensive automotive marketplace (Web & Mobile) in Ecuador. Developed under the company <strong class="focus">Jakay</strong>, Riku digitalizes the entire vehicle buying and selling process, from publication to credit approval and documentation.
  <br/><br/>
  Key architectural modules and modernization initiatives include:
  <br/><br/>
  <strong>Stabilization of Core Workflows:</strong> Rescued and optimized critical transacting workflows built on legacy Angular and Express. This structural stabilization resolved severe transactional bugs in production, saving massive business costs and avoiding high-risk complete rewrites.
  <br/><br/>
  <strong>Banking & Bureau Integrations:</strong> Successfully implemented integrations with Ecuadorian banks and the <strong>Avalburó credit bureau</strong>, <strong class="focus">automating the background vetting process</strong> for car financing applications.
  <br/><br/>
  <strong>Nginx-Based Framework Migration:</strong> Configured a reverse proxy routing strategy in Nginx. This routing <strong class="focus">dynamically resolves URLs</strong>: serving pages from the modern Astro frontend when available, and routing unfinished or legacy paths to the old Angular setup, making a page-by-page rewrite possible <strong class="focus">without any service downtime</strong>.
  <br/><br/>
  <strong>Checkout Price Security:</strong> Implemented a "frozen base-price" model to prevent downstream database edits from corrupting active credit contracts and purchasing tickets.
  <br/><br/>
  <strong>Infrastructure Operations:</strong> Taken charge of building, compiling, and pushing changes to servers running <strong class="focus">Docker containers on Linux</strong>.
  <br/><br/>
  To learn more about the parent fintech development company behind this platform, check out the [Jakay Company Profile](/projects/jakay).
